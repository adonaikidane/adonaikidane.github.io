Rice University, Houston TX. Usually in the middle of three projects, a conversation about markets, and a half-eaten meal.

I'm a sophomore at Rice University studying Computer Science and Statistics — which is a fancy way of saying I spend my time thinking about data, systems, and how to make them do interesting things.

My path took a sharp turn toward quantitative finance when I realized markets are just the world's most chaotic dataset. Now I'm heading to Jane Street as a Focus Fellow, which still feels unreal if I'm honest.

I've shipped AI legal tools, built fraud detection systems, done ML research on edge hardware, and won a hackathon with Claude. I like problems that feel impossible — the kind where you're not sure if you'll figure it out until suddenly you do.

Personality-wise: warm, indecisive in the best way, organized when it counts, and genuinely excited about almost everything. I'm a Martel College rep, in NSBE, the Data Science Club, Robotics, and somehow also Badminton and Powerlifting.
