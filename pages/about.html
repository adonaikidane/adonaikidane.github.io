Houston, TX. Usually thinking about markets, building something, or planning the next trip.

I'm a software engineer with a deep pull toward quantitative finance — the kind of person who finds options pricing genuinely exciting and thinks latency optimization is a form of art.

My work lives at intersections: math and systems, theory and production, rigor and creativity. I've built everything from statistical arbitrage engines to full-stack dashboards that make market data feel alive. Right now I'm open to roles at the intersection of quant and SWE — think trading firms, fintech, or anything where the stakes feel real.
